Help

Common questions

Is a reported market price a guaranteed sale price?

No. It is a dated market observation for reference. A buyer may offer a different price and terms.

Do I need an account to check reported prices?

No account is needed to browse the public price explorer. A current price may not be available for every crop or market.

Can I see every buyer’s personal contact details?

No. The public directory shows only business profiles whose business review is complete. Personal contact details are not published there.

How can I ask for help?

Sign in to raise and track a support request. A public phone or email is shown only if one is configured on the Contact page.

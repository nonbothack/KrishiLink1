## Interpretation

`VERIFIED WORKING` is limited to the exact scope and environment tested. `UNREACHABLE` means implementation exists but routing/navigation does not expose it. `EXTERNAL DEPENDENCY` means product behavior requires provider approval/credentials. `SECURITY/PRODUCTION BLOCKER` marks functionality unsafe to expose as production truth or data. Absence of a status does not imply production readiness.

# Packet 16 research plan (pre-registered)

## Objective

Measure whether the existing release candidate helps real farmers, buyers and FPO operators complete the core journeys with less uncertainty and effort. This plan separates observed evidence from interpretation and treats abstention as valid.

## Hypotheses and thresholds

These thresholds are registered before sessions. They are pilot decision rules, not claims about the population.

| ID | Hypothesis | Measure | Initial threshold |
|---|---|---|---|
| H1 | A farmer can identify the latest relevant reported price and its source/date. | Comprehension pass on price task | ≥80% of valid farmer sessions, n≥5 |
| H2 | A farmer can start and review a lot without unsafe assumptions. | Successful review with canonical crop/unit/quantity | ≥80%, n≥5 |
| H3 | Returning farmers can publish with low effort when real defaults exist. | Meaningful actions from Home to publish, reported by session | Median ≤5, n≥5; no “three taps” claim unless measured |
| H4 | Buyers can create a requirement and evaluate an offer without seeing unnecessary farmer data. | Task success + privacy observation | ≥80%, n≥3 and zero P0/P1 privacy failures |
| H5 | FPO staff can trace intake → pool → lot → trade → member allocation. | Correct trace at every link | 100% of observed trace tasks, n≥2 |
| H6 | Core journeys remain usable under realistic mobile constraints. | Completion by device/network segment | Report by segment; no aggregate claim below n=3 per segment |
| H7 | Support reasons are identifiable from real sessions. | Coded support/error reasons | Descriptive only until n≥10 |

## Panel and recruitment

Target a paid, consented panel: 6–10 farmers, 3–5 buyers and 2–4 FPO operators, with variation in English/Hindi/Marathi, Android capability, device size, connectivity and relevant crop/business context. Recruitment, compensation, conflict-of-interest screening and language review are external operational work. Do not substitute synthetic personas.

## Ethics and safety

Use plain-language consent covering purpose, voluntary participation, recording, withdrawal, retention, compensation, support contact and deletion. Use staging accounts and synthetic transaction fixtures. Never collect OTPs, passwords, bank details, precise GPS, private buyer/farmer contact details or real payment instructions. A moderator may stop a task for distress, privacy exposure, financial risk or unsafe authorization behavior.

## Session design

Run a short orientation, role tasks, a five-second first-impression test for farmers, retrospective comprehension questions and a debrief. Record task outcome, elapsed time, assistance, observed error, evidence reference and severity. Avoid leading participants toward the expected answer. Test at 360×640 and 390×844 where possible, plus 768×1024 and 1440×900; record network and language.

## Stop and escalation rules

Stop the pilot and escalate a P0 for cross-account data, fabricated financial/price claims, unauthorized mutation, unsafe payment language or a participant being asked to use real money. P1 usability blockers are entered into the backlog with evidence and retested before a release recommendation.

## Data handling

Raw recordings and contact data remain in the approved research system and are not committed. Repository artifacts contain participant codes and aggregate/derived evidence only. Every finding must identify build, role, task and session codes so it can be audited.

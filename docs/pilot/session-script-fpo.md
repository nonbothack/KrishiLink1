# FPO moderated-session script

Use an FPO-scoped staging account with multiple synthetic members and receipts.

1. Review the member roster and explain the difference between an FPO member and a platform login.
2. Record an intake with gross, tare and net quantity. Correct a receipt through the supported audit path and explain why the original remains visible.
3. Contribute receipts from multiple members to a pool. State the remaining balance and try an over-contribution in staging.
4. Publish a pooled lot and verify its authoritative quantity equals persisted contributions.
5. Follow an offer through counter/accept into the same Trade Room used by the buyer. Explain the agreed terms and current payment/evidence state.
6. Open the trace: trade → pool → contributions → exact intakes → members → projected allocation. Reconcile quantities and any money rounding.
7. Attempt to access another FPO’s member, intake, pool, offer or trace; record the safe denial without exposing details.

Debrief on receipt terminology, reconciliation, mobile readability, correction safety and support needs.
